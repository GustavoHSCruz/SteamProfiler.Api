# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors and the project. Report conduct concerns through
the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
